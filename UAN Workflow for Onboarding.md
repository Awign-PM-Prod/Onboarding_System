# **Step-by-Step Process Guide**

# **A. If Employee Already Has a UAN Number**

## **Step 1 — Enter Existing UAN Details**

Employee must enter:

* Existing UAN Number  
* Aadhaar-linked Mobile Number  
* Aadhaar Details (if required)

---

## **Step 2 — Download Required Applications**

Install:

* UMANG App  
* Aadhaar FaceRD App

using the links provided above.

---

## **Step 3 — Complete UAN Activation**

### **Open the UMANG App**

### **First-Time Users**

* Click on Register  
* Enter Aadhaar-linked mobile number  
* Verify using OTP

### **Existing Users**

* Login using:  
  * MPIN, or  
  * Mobile Number with OTP verification

---

## **Step 4 — Navigate to EPFO Services**

* Search for:  
   **EPFO**  
* Open:  
   **UAN Services Through Face Auth**

---

## **Step 5 — Select Face Authentication**

Click on:

**“Face Authentication of Already Activated UANs”**

---

## **Step 6 — Complete Face Authentication**

* Click Start  
* Allow camera permissions  
* Blink eyes when prompted  
* Ensure proper lighting during authentication

Once completed successfully, the following message will appear:

“Aadhaar Face Authentication is successful.”

---

## **Step 7 — Upload Confirmation Screenshot**

Employee must upload:

* Screenshot showing successful face authentication

---

## **Step 8 — Submit**

Click Submit to complete the onboarding process.

---

# **B. If Employee Does NOT Have a UAN Number**

## **Step 1 — Download Required Applications**

Install:

* UMANG App  
* Aadhaar FaceRD App

using the links provided above.

---

## **Step 2 — Open UMANG App**

Register/Login using Aadhaar-linked mobile number.

---

## **Step 3 — Start UAN Activation Process**

Navigate to:

* EPFO Services  
* UAN Activation

---

## **Step 4 — Enter Required Details**

Employee must enter:

* Aadhaar Number  
* Mobile Number linked with Aadhaar  
* Other requested details

---

## **Step 5 — Aadhaar Verification**

* Tick consent checkbox for Aadhaar validation  
* Click Send OTP  
* Enter OTP received on registered mobile number

---

## **Step 6 — UAN Generation**

Upon successful verification:

* UAN will be generated successfully  
* UAN details will be received via SMS on registered mobile number

---

## **Step 7 — Enter Generated UAN Details**

Employee must enter the generated UAN details in the onboarding portal/process.

---

## **Step 8 — Complete Face Authentication**

Follow the same face authentication process through:

* UMANG App  
* Aadhaar FaceRD App

---

## **Step 9 — Upload Confirmation Screenshot**

Employee must upload:

* Screenshot showing successful face authentication

---

## **Step 10 — Submit**

Click Submit to complete the onboarding process.

---

# **Important Notes**

* Mobile number must be linked with Aadhaar.  
* Stable internet connection is recommended.  
* Ensure camera permissions are enabled.  
* Face authentication should be completed in proper lighting conditions.  
* Employees are advised to keep Aadhaar details readily available before starting the process.

---

# **Employee Submission Requirement**

Before submission, employees must ensure:

✅ UAN is generated/activated  
 ✅ Face authentication is completed successfully  
 ✅ Screenshot proof is uploaded  
 ✅ All required details are entered correctly

---

# **Support Guidance**

Employees facing issues during:

* UAN Generation  
* OTP Verification  
* UMANG Login  
* Face Authentication

should contact the onboarding/support team for assistance.
